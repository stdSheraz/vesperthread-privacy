Vesperthread: The Five Silent Bells – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Vesperthread: The Five Silent Bells ("the Game", package com.vesperthread.game) handles information. The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over.

Information the Game itself stores

The Game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. Game progress (unlocked stages, Song Fragments, coins, upgrades and settings) is saved only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Advertising

The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), an ad mediation service, and its ad network partners. To serve, measure and limit the frequency of ads and to prevent fraud, these providers may automatically collect:

  • the device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID) and app set ID,
  • IP address, approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device and operating-system information (model, OS version, language, screen size, network type),
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed or clicked).

This data is processed by the ad providers under their own privacy policies, including the Unity / ironSource privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Ads between stages

From stage 4 onward the Game may occasionally show a full-screen ad when you leave a stage-clear screen (at most every third cleared stage, and not more than once every few minutes).

Rewarded ads

Some optional features (an extra life, doubling stage coins or bonus Prism Shards) let you choose to watch a video ad. Watching is always optional.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising providers described above, no data is shared with third parties.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
